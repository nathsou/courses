# Source coverage and model provenance

Checked 4 October 2026. This is a compact editorial ledger for the delivered course. It records the high-impact claims and the complete topic map, rather than pretending that every explanatory sentence is a separately verified primary-research claim. The bibliography’s DOI metadata, titles, authors and online/volume dates were checked against Crossref. The six caption reading copies were sampled throughout, with closer review of the passages used for activities and revisions. No recording was listened to and no lecture slide was used as a source for numerical data.
